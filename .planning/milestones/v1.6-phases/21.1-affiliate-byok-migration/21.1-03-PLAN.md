<objective>
Thread an optional `openRouterApiKey` parameter through the three remaining text-call services — caption quality, carousel master plan, and enhancement pre-screen/caption — mirroring the `imageApiKey?: string` precedent those same interfaces already established for the OpenAI image provider.

Purpose: These three services hold four of the six gateway text call sites that currently hardcode the platform key. Without them, an affiliate's carousel generation and product-photo enhancement would still bill the platform even after `gemini.service.ts` is fixed.
Output: Three service interfaces that accept a per-request OpenRouter key. Routes supply it in Wave 3 (plans 04-06).
</objective>
