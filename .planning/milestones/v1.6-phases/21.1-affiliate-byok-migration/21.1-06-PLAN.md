<objective>
Close the last two affiliate-facing AI surfaces: `POST /api/transcribe` (voice input) and `POST /api/posts/:id/remake-caption`. Both currently hard-gate on `profile.api_key` (Gemini) and would therefore 400 every affiliate who configures only an OpenRouter key — the Settings Gemini field becomes optional and video-scoped in plan 07, and neither of these routes has any video or direct-Google path that needs it.

Purpose: `transcribe.routes.ts:141` still reads `config.OPENROUTER_API_KEY` directly, ignoring the per-user key already resolved earlier in the same file. `posts.routes.ts:362-382` is a seventh call site that 21.1-RESEARCH's per-file table did not enumerate — it duplicates the Phase 12.3 tier-model gate inline and is reachable by affiliates from the post viewer's "remake caption" action.
Output: All seven affiliate-facing AI surfaces resolve and use the affiliate's own OpenRouter key.
</objective>
