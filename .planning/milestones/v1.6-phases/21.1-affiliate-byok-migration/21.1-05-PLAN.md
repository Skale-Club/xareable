<objective>
Rewire the carousel routes (generation + per-slide edit) and the enhancement route onto affiliate-aware key resolution, threading the affiliate's OpenRouter key into `generateCarousel`, `enhanceProductPhoto`, and both image-provider call sites.

Purpose: These three handlers hold the remaining SSE-streaming AI surfaces. Without them, an affiliate's carousel (which makes 1 planning call + N image calls + 1 caption call) and their enhancement (pre-screen + image + caption) would still bill the platform's OpenRouter balance.
Output: All three handlers resolve, gate on, and thread the affiliate's OpenRouter key.
