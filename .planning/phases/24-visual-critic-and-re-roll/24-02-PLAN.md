<objective>
Make the gateway capable of two things it cannot do today: (1) run a chat completion under a
caller-specified call class, and (2) be genuinely cancelled mid-flight.

Purpose: `chatCompletion()` hardcodes its fallback call class to `"text"` in TWO places
(24-RESEARCH Pitfall 1), so wiring the critic through it unmodified would silently share the
planning/caption/pre-screen fallback chain — defeating 24-CONTEXT.md's locked "separate call
class" decision. And no `fetch()`/SDK call in this codebase has ever received a real
`AbortSignal` (24-RESEARCH Pitfall 3) — the existing carousel/enhance pattern is cooperative-only
and would NOT satisfy CRIT-04's literal wording ("the wired AbortSignal actually cancels the
in-flight call").
Output: two additive optional params on the gateway, threaded to the real network calls, with
100% backward compatibility for all 6 existing `chatCompletion` callers.
</objective>
